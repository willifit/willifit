How tall is a U-Haul, box truck, van, or RV?

Sources checked Sep 10, 2026 · Updated Sep 10, 2026

U-Haul lists a clearance height of 7 ft for its pickup, 8 ft for its cargo van, 9 ft for its 10 ft truck, 11 ft for its 15, 17, and 20 ft trucks, and 12 ft for its 26 ft truck. Budget lists 8'9" for its cargo van, 9'0" for its 12 ft truck, 11'0" for its 16 ft truck, and 13' for its 26 ft truck. Penske publishes an exterior clearance height only for its 26 ft truck: 13 ft 6 in. Most U.S. parking garages are built to a 7'0" clear height, with 8'2" (98 inches) required on van-accessible routes, so no rental box truck fits inside a typical garage; the U-Haul pickup fits a 7'0" garage with no margin, and the U-Haul cargo van needs the 8'2" route.

How tall is a semi truck?

There is no federal maximum vehicle height. The Federal Highway Administration says states set their own limits, typically between 13'6" and 14' on the National Network. 13'6" is the common design figure for trailers, not a federal rule.

What clearance do parking garages have?

Most U.S. building codes derive their parking-garage clear height from the International Building Code, which requires at least 7 feet (84 inches) of clear height in vehicle and pedestrian traffic areas (IBC §406.2.2 as adopted, via up.codes). Local amendments can raise it.

The ADA Standards for Accessible Design require 98 inches (8'2") of vertical clearance for van-accessible parking spaces, their access aisles, and the vehicle route to and from them (U.S. Access Board, ADA Standards §502.5).

See clearance by garage type on the parking garage clearance guide.

Will my truck fit in a parking garage?

Only if it is a pickup, SUV, or low-roof van. Against the two standard garage clearances, 7'0" (84 inches) and 8'2" (98 inches): stock pickups and full-size SUVs (75 to 76 in) clear both; low-roof cargo vans (Ford Transit 82.7 in, Chevrolet Express 85 in, Ram ProMaster 93 in) clear the 8'2" route but only the Transit clears 7'0"; every mid-roof or high-roof van (99.8 to 110 in), every rental box truck (9 ft and up), and every motorhome class (8.5 ft and up) is too tall for both.

A U-Haul pickup (7 ft) fits a 7'0" garage with no margin, and a U-Haul cargo van (8 ft) fits only under an 8'2" van-accessible route. Budget's cargo van (8'9") does not fit either.

Individual garages post their own limits, which run from under 6 ft to more than 9 ft. Enter your vehicle's height on the interactive map, or open the city page for where you are going, to see each garage's posted clearance.

What these numbers leave out

  • Roof-mounted equipment. Air conditioners, vents, satellite dishes, solar panels, antennas, and roof racks are not included in published heights.
  • Lift kits and larger tires. Any lift or tire change raises a vehicle above its stock specification.
  • Load. Most published figures are unloaded; suspension compresses under load, so a loaded vehicle is usually slightly lower, not higher.
  • Different labels. "Clearance height" (U-Haul, Budget) and "overall height" (Ford, Mercedes-Benz) are different measurement conventions; no vendor states what margin, if any, is built in.
  • Missing data. Penske, Enterprise, and Home Depot do not publish exterior heights for most of their rental trucks; those rows say so rather than guess.

Leave yourself margin. A posted clearance is the lowest point on a beam or pipe that may sag, ice up, or sit lower than the sign suggests. We recommend keeping at least 6 inches between your true height and the posted clearance — and never trusting a number you didn't read off the sign yourself. A wrong guess costs a torn-off A/C unit, a peeled roof, or worse.

Frequently asked questions

How tall is a 15 ft U-Haul truck?

U-Haul lists an 11 ft clearance height for its 15 ft truck, the same figure as its 17 ft and 20 ft trucks. It will not fit in a parking garage; typical garage clearance is 7'0" to 8'2".

How tall is a 26 ft U-Haul truck?

12 ft clearance height, per U-Haul. Budget's 26 ft truck is listed at 13' and Penske's at 13 ft 6 in, so check the brand, not just the length.

How tall is a 10 ft U-Haul truck?

9 ft clearance height, per U-Haul. That is taller than any standard parking garage, which is 7'0" typical and 8'2" on van-accessible routes.

Will a U-Haul cargo van fit in a parking garage?

U-Haul lists an 8 ft clearance height for its cargo van. It clears an 8'2" (98 inch) van-accessible route with 2 inches to spare but not a 7'0" garage. Budget's cargo van is listed at 8'9" and does not fit either.

How tall is a Penske truck?

Penske publishes interior cargo heights for its 12, 16, and 22 ft trucks and an exterior clearance height only for the 26 ft truck: 13 ft 6 in. None of them fits in a parking garage.

Will a high-roof Sprinter or Transit fit in a parking garage?

Ford lists the high-roof Transit at 110 in (9'2") and Mercedes-Benz lists the high-roof Sprinter at 107 in (8'11"); both exceed the 8'2" van-accessible minimum. The low-roof Transit, at 82.7 in (about 6'11"), clears a 7'0" garage with about an inch to spare.

How tall is a Class C RV?

About 10 to 11 ft before roof-mounted air conditioners and vents, per rvshare. No standard parking garage clears that; use a surface lot.

How tall is a semi truck?

There is no federal height limit. States set their own, typically 13'6" to 14' on the National Network, according to the Federal Highway Administration. 13'6" is the common trailer design figure, not a federal rule.

Will my truck fit in a parking garage?

A stock pickup or full-size SUV fits: published heights of 75 to 76 in clear both the 7'0" (84 in) typical garage clearance and the 8'2" (98 in) van-accessible minimum. Rental box trucks start at 9 ft and do not fit any standard garage. Check the posted clearance of the specific garage; they range from under 6 ft to over 9 ft.
