What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Gatlinburg, Tennessee?

The lowest clearance on file for Gatlinburg garages is 8'0" (96 inches) at Hollywood Wax Museum. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'9") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
