What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Scottsdale, Arizona?
The lowest clearance on file for Scottsdale garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Scottsdale Civic Center Mall Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Scottsdale accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

Are there RV parks in Scottsdale?

How is the clearance data verified?
