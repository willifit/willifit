What's the highest-clearance parking option in Portland?
Unnamed parking structure has the tallest verified clearance in Portland at 12'8" (152 inches), which accommodates most box trucks and smaller RVs.
Which parking facilities in Portland accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
11 facilities in Portland are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Moda Center — Benton Lot (Oversized), OMSI Parking, Washington Park (Zoo/Arboretum) and 8 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#portland-or.
Are there RV parks in Portland?
Yes. 2 RV parks are indexed in Portland, including Clackamas RV Park, Roamers Rest RV Park. RV parks have no overhead clearance — any vehicle size fits.
How is the clearance data verified?
3 of the 161 locations on this page are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 4 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 154 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
