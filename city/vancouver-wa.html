Frequently asked

Which parking facilities in Vancouver accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

13 facilities in Vancouver are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: 99th Street Transit Center, Fairground Chevron RV Park, Delta Park/Vanport Park and Ride and 10 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#vancouver-wa.

Are there RV parks in Vancouver?

Yes. 10 RV parks are indexed in Vancouver, including Fairground Chevron RV Park, 99 RV Park, Janzen Beach RV Park and 7 more. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.

How is the clearance data verified?

The 50 clearances on this page are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory. They have not yet been individually verified against Street View, so treat them as a starting point and always confirm at the posted sign before you drive. Other cities on WillIFit.ai include AI-verified readings taken directly from the entrance sign.
