What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Vancouver, Washington?
The lowest verified clearance in Vancouver is 6'0" (72 inches) at Lovejoy Medical Parking. Standard cars and small SUVs fit, but vans, RVs, and box trucks should look elsewhere.
What's the highest-clearance parking option in Vancouver?
PMC has the tallest verified clearance in Vancouver at 6'6" (78 inches), which accommodates most box trucks and smaller RVs.
Which parking facilities in Vancouver accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
13 facilities in Vancouver are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: 99th Street Transit Center, Fairground Chevron RV Park, Delta Park/Vanport Park and Ride and 10 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#vancouver-wa.
Are there RV parks in Vancouver?
Yes. 10 RV park(s) are indexed in Vancouver, including Fairground Chevron RV Park, 99 RV Park, Janzen Beach RV Park and 7 more. RV parks have no overhead clearance — any vehicle size fits.
How is the clearance data verified?
