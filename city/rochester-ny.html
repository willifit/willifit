Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Rochester, New York?

The lowest clearance on file for Rochester garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at South Avenue Garage. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Rochester accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

9 facilities in Rochester are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Public Parking, ABVI Goodwill, Bushnell's Basin Park and Ride Lot and 6 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#rochester-ny.

How is the clearance data verified?

The 104 clearances on this page are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory. They have not yet been individually verified against Street View, so treat them as a starting point and always confirm at the posted sign before you drive. Other cities on WillIFit.ai include AI-verified readings taken directly from the entrance sign.
