Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Sacramento, California?

The lowest clearance on file for Sacramento garages is 6'9" (81 inches) at Golden 1 Center — DOCO Garage. It was verified against golden1center.com on Apr 27, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Sacramento?

The highest clearance on file for Sacramento garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at SMF — Terminal A Garage. It was verified against sacramento.aero on Apr 27, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Sacramento accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

20 facilities in Sacramento are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Old Sacramento Historic District Lot, William Land Park / Zoo Lots, California State Railroad Museum Lot and 17 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#sacramento-ca.

Are there RV parks in Sacramento?

Yes. 4 RV parks are indexed in Sacramento, including Sacramento Sun RV Park, SacWest RV Park & Campground, Cal Expo RV Park and 1 more. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.

How is the clearance data verified?
