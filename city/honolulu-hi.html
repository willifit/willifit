Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Honolulu, Hawaii?

The lowest clearance on file for Honolulu garages is 6'2" (74 inches) at Bank of Hawaii. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Honolulu?

The highest clearance on file for Honolulu garages is 14'0" (168 inches) at International Market Place (Waikiki). It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears every common vehicle class, including a 13'6" semi trailer. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Honolulu accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

Are there RV parks in Honolulu?
