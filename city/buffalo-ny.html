Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Buffalo, New York?

The lowest clearance on file for Buffalo garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Walden Galleria Parking Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Jul 31, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Buffalo?

The highest clearance on file for Buffalo garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at Buffalo AKG Art Museum. It was verified against buffaloakg.org on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'9") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
