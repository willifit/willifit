Which parking facilities in Fort Lauderdale accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

Are there RV parks in Fort Lauderdale?

How is the clearance data verified?

The 54 clearances on this page are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory.
