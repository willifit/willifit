What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Fresno, California?
The lowest clearance on file for Fresno garages is 12'0" (144 inches) at Fresno Convention Center / Saroyan Theatre. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a Class C RV (11'6") and anything shorter, but not a 26 ft rental truck (13'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
