Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Greensboro, North Carolina?
The lowest clearance on file for Greensboro garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Deep Roots Market District. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Greensboro accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
9 facilities in Greensboro are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Greensboro Coliseum Complex, Greensboro Science Center + Animal Discovery, Natural Science Center of Greensboro and 6 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#greensboro-nc.
Are there RV parks in Greensboro?
Yes. 1 RV park is indexed in Greensboro, including KOA Kampground. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.
