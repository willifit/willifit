Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in El Paso, Texas?
The lowest clearance on file for El Paso garages is 6'5" (77 inches) at El Paso Convention Center Garage. It was verified against elpasolive.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in El Paso?
The highest clearance on file for El Paso garages is 16'11" (203 inches) at Sun Bowl Parking Garage (SBG). It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 24, 2026. That clears every common vehicle class, including a 13'6" semi trailer. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in El Paso accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
9 facilities in El Paso are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Plaza Theatre / Hotel Paso del Norte Lot, Sun Bowl Stadium Lots, Don Haskins Center Lots and 6 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#el-paso-tx.
Are there RV parks in El Paso?
Yes. 2 RV parks are indexed in El Paso, including Ft Bliss RV Park, Gaslight Square RV Park. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.
