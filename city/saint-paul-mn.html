The lowest verified clearance in Saint Paul is 6'8" (80 inches) at Northrop Garage. Standard cars and small SUVs fit, but vans, RVs, and box trucks should look elsewhere.
What's the highest-clearance parking option in Saint Paul?
Gortner Avenue Parking Ramp has the tallest verified clearance in Saint Paul at 8'2" (98 inches), which accommodates most box trucks and smaller RVs.
Which parking facilities in Saint Paul accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

How is the clearance data verified?
