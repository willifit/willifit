What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Albuquerque, New Mexico?

The lowest clearance on file for Albuquerque garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Albuquerque Sunport (ABQ) Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Jul 3, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Albuquerque?

The highest clearance on file for Albuquerque garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Albuquerque Convention Center Garage. It was verified against en.parkopedia.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Are there RV parks in Albuquerque?
