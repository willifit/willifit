Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Portland, Maine?
The lowest verified clearance in Portland is 6'6" (78 inches) at Elm Street Parking Facility. Standard cars and small SUVs fit, but vans, RVs, and box trucks should look elsewhere.
What's the highest-clearance parking option in Portland?
USM Surrenden Street Parking Garage has the tallest verified clearance in Portland at 8'2" (98 inches), which accommodates most box trucks and smaller RVs.
Which parking facilities in Portland accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
9 facilities in Portland are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Wild Acres, Paradise Park Resort, Westbrook Park and Ride and 6 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#portland-me.
Are there RV parks in Portland?
Yes. 2 RV parks are indexed in Portland, including Pinehirst RV Resort, Wild Duck Campground & RV Park. RV parks have no overhead clearance — any vehicle size fits.
How is the clearance data verified?
1 of the 92 locations on this page are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. The remaining 91 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
