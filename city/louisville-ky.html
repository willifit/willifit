What's the highest-clearance parking option in Louisville?
Le petit Parking has the tallest verified clearance in Louisville at 9'10" (118 inches), which accommodates most box trucks and smaller RVs.

How is the clearance data verified?
2 of the 112 locations on this page are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 7 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 103 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
