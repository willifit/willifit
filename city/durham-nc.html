What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Durham, North Carolina?
The lowest clearance on file for Durham garages is 6'3" (75 inches) at Durham Centre Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Durham?
The highest clearance on file for Durham garages is 7'4" (88 inches) at Church Street Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 24, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
