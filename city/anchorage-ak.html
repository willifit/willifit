Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Anchorage, Alaska?
The lowest clearance on file for Anchorage garages is 6'7" (79 inches) at JC Penney Parking Garage. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Anchorage?
The highest clearance on file for Anchorage garages is 7'3" (87 inches) at Anchorage Museum Parking Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 23, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Are there RV parks in Anchorage?

How is the clearance data verified?
