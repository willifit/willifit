Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Colorado Springs, Colorado?
The lowest clearance on file for Colorado Springs garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Antlers Hotel Garage. It was verified against antlers.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Colorado Springs?
The highest clearance on file for Colorado Springs garages is 13'5" (161 inches) at Chapel Hills Mall. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a Class C RV (11'6") and anything shorter, but not a 26 ft rental truck (13'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Colorado Springs accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
26 facilities in Colorado Springs are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: USAFA — Visitor Center Lot, Garden of the Gods Visitor Center, Garden of the Gods — Main Parking Lots and 23 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#colorado-springs-co.
Are there RV parks in Colorado Springs?
Yes. 6 RV parks are indexed in Colorado Springs, including RV Park (unnamed), Pikes Peak RV Park, Foot of the Rockies RV Resort and 3 more. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.
