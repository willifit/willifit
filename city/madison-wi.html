Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Madison, Wisconsin?
The lowest clearance on file for Madison garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Overture Center Ramp. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Madison?
The highest clearance on file for Madison garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at Urban Land Interests. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Jul 31, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Madison accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
