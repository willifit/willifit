What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Charleston, South Carolina?
The lowest clearance on file for Charleston garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Queen Street Parking Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on May 1, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Charleston?
The highest clearance on file for Charleston garages is 10'10" (130 inches) at Metropolis Technologies, Inc.. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 24, 2026. That clears a Class B camper van (9'8") and anything shorter, but not a 15–20 ft rental truck (11'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
