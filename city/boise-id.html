Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Boise, Idaho?

The lowest clearance on file for Boise garages is 7'10" (94 inches) at Myrtle Street Parking Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 1, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Boise accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

15 facilities in Boise are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Zoo Boise Lot (Julia Davis Park), Julia Davis Park Lots, Ann Morrison Park Lots and 12 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#boise-id.

Are there RV parks in Boise?

Yes. 3 RV parks are indexed in Boise, including RV Park (unnamed), ​Boise Riverside RV Park, Hi Valley RV Park. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.
