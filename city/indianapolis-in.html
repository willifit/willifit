Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Indianapolis, Indiana?

The lowest clearance on file for Indianapolis garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Indiana Convention Center — Sports Garage. It was verified against generalparking.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Indianapolis?

The highest clearance on file for Indianapolis garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at IND Airport — Terminal Garage. It was verified against ind.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'9") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Indianapolis accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

13 facilities in Indianapolis are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Lucas Oil Stadium — Blue Lot, Lucas Oil Stadium — Orange Lot, Indianapolis Motor Speedway — Lots and 10 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#indianapolis-in.
