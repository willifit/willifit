Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Salt Lake City, Utah?

The lowest clearance on file for Salt Lake City garages is 7'4" (88 inches) at Delta Center (Jazz) Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Jul 4, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Salt Lake City?

The highest clearance on file for Salt Lake City garages is 8'6" (102 inches) at SLC Airport — Terminal Garage (new). It was verified against slcairport.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") and anything shorter, but not a 10–12 ft rental truck (9'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Salt Lake City accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

11 facilities in Salt Lake City are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Rice-Eccles Stadium (Utes), This Is The Place Heritage Park, Hogle Zoo and 8 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#salt-lake-ut.

Are there RV parks in Salt Lake City?
