Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Des Moines, Iowa?
The lowest verified clearance in Des Moines is 7'0" (84 inches) at 4th & Grand Parking Garage. Standard cars and small SUVs fit, but vans, RVs, and box trucks should look elsewhere.
What's the highest-clearance parking option in Des Moines?
10th Street Parkade has the tallest verified clearance in Des Moines at 7'4" (88 inches), which accommodates most box trucks and smaller RVs.
Which parking facilities in Des Moines accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
14 facilities in Des Moines are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Des Moines Civic Center, Science Center of Iowa, Des Moines Art Center and 11 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#des-moines-ia.
Are there RV parks in Des Moines?
Yes. 2 RV park(s) are indexed in Des Moines, including Griff's Valley View RV Park, RV Park (unnamed). RV parks have no overhead clearance — any vehicle size fits.
How is the clearance data verified?
3 of the 72 locations on this page are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 5 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 64 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
⚠ Always verify at the sign. Posted clearances on this page are for planning. The only authoritative number is the sign at the garage entrance or bridge approach.
