Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Tucson, Arizona?

The lowest clearance on file for Tucson garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at El Presidio Parking Garage. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Tucson?

The highest clearance on file for Tucson garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Centro Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Tucson accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

Are there RV parks in Tucson?

Yes. 3 RV parks are indexed in Tucson, including Tucson / Lazydays KOA, Casino del Sol RV Park, Prince of Tucson RV Park. RV parks are open-air sites without a garage ceiling; check each park's own length and height limits.
