What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Springfield, Illinois?
The lowest clearance on file for Springfield garages is 12'11" (155 inches) at Municipal Parking Deck. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 1, 2026. That clears a Class C RV (11'6") and anything shorter, but not a 26 ft rental truck (13'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Springfield accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

How is the clearance data verified?
1 of the 84 locations on this page are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. The remaining 83 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
