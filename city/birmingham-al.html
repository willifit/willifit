Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Birmingham, Alabama?

The lowest clearance on file for Birmingham garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at McWane Science Center. It was verified against mcwane.org on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Birmingham?

The highest clearance on file for Birmingham garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at BJCC (Birmingham-Jefferson Convention Complex). It was verified against bjcc.org on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Birmingham accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
