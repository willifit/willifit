Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Milwaukee, Wisconsin?

The lowest clearance on file for Milwaukee garages is 6'6" (78 inches) at Wisconsin Center Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 23, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Milwaukee?

The highest clearance on file for Milwaukee garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at Fiserv Forum — Event Garages. It was verified against fiservforum.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which parking facilities in Milwaukee accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

29 facilities in Milwaukee are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: American Family Field Lots, Summerfest / Henry Maier Festival Park Lots, Milwaukee County Zoo and 26 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#milwaukee-wi.

How is the clearance data verified?
