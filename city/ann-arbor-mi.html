Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Ann Arbor, Michigan?
The lowest clearance on file for Ann Arbor garages is 6'6" (78 inches) at Fourth & Washington Parking Structure. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Ann Arbor accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
1 facility in Ann Arbor is explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Lot C: Public. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#ann-arbor-mi.
How is the clearance data verified?
The 75 clearances on this page are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory. They have not yet been individually verified against Street View, so treat them as a starting point and always confirm at the posted sign before you drive. Other cities on WillIFit.ai include AI-verified readings taken directly from the entrance sign.
