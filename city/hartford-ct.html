What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Hartford, Connecticut?

That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Hartford?
The highest clearance on file for Hartford garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at Bushnell Performing Arts Center. It was verified against propark.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
