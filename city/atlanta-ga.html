Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Atlanta, Georgia?
The lowest clearance on file for Atlanta garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Mercedes-Benz Stadium — Green/Red Lots. It was verified against mercedesbenzstadium.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Atlanta?
The highest clearance on file for Atlanta garages is 13'3" (159 inches) at Ponce City Market Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 1, 2026. That clears a Class C RV (11'6") and anything shorter, but not a 26 ft rental truck (13'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Atlanta accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
38 facilities in Atlanta are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: State Farm Arena Parking Deck, National Center for Civil & Human Rights, ATL — ATL West / Economy Lot and 35 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#atlanta-ga.
