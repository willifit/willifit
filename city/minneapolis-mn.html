Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Minneapolis, Minnesota?
The lowest clearance on file for Minneapolis garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Northrop Garage. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Minneapolis?
The highest clearance on file for Minneapolis garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at Gortner Avenue Parking Ramp. It is imported from OpenStreetMap and not yet individually verified. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Minneapolis accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
4 facilities in Minneapolis are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Mall of America — East/West Ramps, Minnehaha Falls Park Lots, MSP — Quick Ride Long-Term and 1 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#minneapolis-mn.
How is the clearance data verified?
