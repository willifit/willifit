What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Orlando, Florida?

The lowest clearance on file for Orlando garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Universal — Prime Self-Parking. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Orlando?

The highest clearance on file for Orlando garages is 15'0" (180 inches) at MCO Airport — Terminal A Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears every common vehicle class, including a 13'6" semi trailer. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
