Which parking facilities in Tupelo accept RVs or oversized vehicles?

None of the parking facilities currently indexed in Tupelo are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly. RV and box-truck drivers should call ahead, look for surface lots, or check nearby cities on willifit.ai.

How is the clearance data verified?

The 33 clearances on this page are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory. They have not yet been individually verified against Street View, so treat them as a starting point and always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
