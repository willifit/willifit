What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Raleigh, North Carolina?

The lowest clearance on file for Raleigh garages is 7'0" (84 inches) at Raleigh Convention Center Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Apr 23, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
