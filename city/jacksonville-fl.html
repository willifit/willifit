What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Jacksonville, Florida?
The lowest clearance on file for Jacksonville garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at JAX Center Garage. The figure comes from ParkMobile and has not been individually re-verified. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Jacksonville?
The highest clearance on file for Jacksonville garages is 13'6" (162 inches) at CityPlace Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Jul 4, 2026. That clears every common vehicle class, including a 13'6" semi trailer. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
