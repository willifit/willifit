What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Tampa, Florida?
The lowest clearance on file for Tampa garages is 6'8" (80 inches) at Amalie Arena (Lightning) Garage. It was verified against amaliearena.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Tampa?
The highest clearance on file for Tampa garages is 8'4" (100 inches) at Water Street Tampa — Heritage & Markel Garages. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 1, 2026. That clears a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") and anything shorter, but not a 10–12 ft rental truck (9'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Tampa accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
10 facilities in Tampa are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Raymond James Stadium (Bucs) Lots, Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, ZooTampa at Lowry Park and 7 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#tampa-fl.
