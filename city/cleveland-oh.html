Frequently asked

What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Cleveland, Ohio?
The lowest clearance on file for Cleveland garages is 6'0" (72 inches) at Tower City Center Garage. It was verified against towercitycenter.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
What's the highest-clearance parking garage in Cleveland?
The highest clearance on file for Cleveland garages is 9'6" (114 inches) at Rocket Mortgage FieldHouse Garage. It was verified against rocketmortgagefieldhouse.com on Apr 30, 2026. That clears a high-roof Sprinter or Transit (9'6") and anything shorter, but not a Class B camper van (9'8") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Cleveland accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
6 facilities in Cleveland are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: Cleveland Browns Stadium (Huntington Bank Field), CLE — Brown Economy Lot, Cleveland Metroparks Zoo and 3 more. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#cleveland-oh.
How is the clearance data verified?
