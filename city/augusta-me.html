What's the lowest-clearance parking garage in Augusta, Maine?
The lowest clearance on file for Augusta garages is 8'2" (98 inches) at Dickman Street Parking Garage. It was AI-verified from Street View signage on Aug 1, 2026. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which parking facilities in Augusta accept RVs or oversized vehicles?
2 facilities in Augusta are explicitly marked as oversized-vehicle-friendly: City Of Augusta, Shaw's. See the full list on the interactive map at willifit.ai/#augusta-me.
