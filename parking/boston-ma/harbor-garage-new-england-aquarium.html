Frequently asked

Will a U-Haul fit at Harbor Garage (New England Aquarium)?

No. The posted clearance is 6'6". U-Haul lists a clearance height of 9'0" for its 10 ft truck, 11'0" for its 15, 17, and 20 ft trucks, and 12'0" for its 26 ft truck. None of them fits under 6'6". Budget and Penske publish different figures; see the vehicle heights guide at willifit.ai/vehicle-heights.html.

Will an RV fit at Harbor Garage (New England Aquarium)?

Class B camper vans are typically 8.5 to 11 ft tall and Class C motorhomes 10 to 11 ft, not counting roof-mounted air conditioners and vents. Neither a Class B camper van nor a Class C motorhome fits under 6'6". Class A motorhomes and fifth wheels run 11 to 13.5 ft and exceed most garage clearances; use a surface lot.
