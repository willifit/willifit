Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in New York?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in the New York metro areas it covers is 6'0" (72 inches) at Tunnel (service) in Niagara Falls. Coverage follows each city's metro area, so a structure just across a state line can appear here. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which New York cities does WillIFit cover?

How is the clearance data verified?

19 of the 1256 locations in New York are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 5 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 1232 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
