Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in New Jersey?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in New Jersey is 6'4" (76 inches) at Tunnel (service) in Jersey City. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which New Jersey cities does WillIFit cover?

WillIFit covers 4 cities in New Jersey: Newark (459 locations), Jersey City (237 locations), Trenton (115 locations), Atlantic City (41 locations).

Where can an RV or box truck park in New Jersey?

19 indexed facilities in New Jersey are marked oversized-vehicle-friendly, including Shady Pines in Atlantic City, Bus Parking in Atlantic City, Liberty State Park Lots in Jersey City. See each city page for the full list.

How is the clearance data verified?

16 of the 852 locations in New Jersey are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 2 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 834 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
