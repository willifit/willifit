Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Pennsylvania?
The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Pennsylvania is 6'3" (75 inches) at Fort Duquesne Bridge (I-279) in Pittsburgh. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which Pennsylvania cities does WillIFit cover?
WillIFit covers 10 cities in Pennsylvania: Philadelphia (425 locations), Pittsburgh (267 locations), Allentown (154 locations), Harrisburg (70 locations), Scranton (58 locations), Lancaster (52 locations), Reading (49 locations), Erie (42 locations), Williamsport (15 locations), Altoona (6 locations).

How is the clearance data verified?
19 of the 1138 locations in Pennsylvania are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 3 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 1116 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
