What is the lowest bridge clearance in Delaware?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in the Delaware metro areas it covers is 9'0" (108 inches) at Low clearance: RIVERWALK PATH under NEW SWEDEN ST in Wilmington. Coverage follows each city's metro area, so a structure just across a state line can appear here. That clears a 10–12 ft rental truck (9'0") and anything shorter, but not a high-roof Sprinter or Transit (9'2") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
