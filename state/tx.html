Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Texas?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in the Texas metro areas it covers is 6'0" (72 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in Houston. Coverage follows each city's metro area, so a structure just across a state line can appear here. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

How is the clearance data verified?

67 of the 3897 locations in Texas are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 18 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 3812 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
