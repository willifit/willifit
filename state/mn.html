Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Minnesota?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Minnesota is 7'0" (84 inches) at Tunnel (service) in Minneapolis. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which Minnesota cities does WillIFit cover?

WillIFit covers 4 cities in Minnesota: Minneapolis (172 locations), Saint Paul (77 locations), Rochester (57 locations), Duluth (14 locations).

Where can an RV or box truck park in Minnesota?

68 indexed facilities in Minnesota are marked oversized-vehicle-friendly, including Duluth Public Schools in Duluth, Mall of America — East/West Ramps in Minneapolis, Minnehaha Falls Park Lots in Minneapolis. See each city page for the full list.

How is the clearance data verified?

4 of the 320 locations in Minnesota are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 4 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 312 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
