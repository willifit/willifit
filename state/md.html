Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Maryland?
The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Maryland is 6'7" (79 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in Baltimore. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'0") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which Maryland cities does WillIFit cover?

How is the clearance data verified?
29 of the 295 locations in Maryland are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 1 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 265 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
