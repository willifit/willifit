Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Massachusetts?
The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Massachusetts is 6'0" (72 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in Boston. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

4 of the 630 locations in Massachusetts are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself.
