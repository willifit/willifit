Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Nevada?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in the Nevada metro areas it covers is 6'0" (72 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in Las Vegas. Coverage follows each city's metro area, so a structure just across a state line can appear here. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which Nevada cities does WillIFit cover?

WillIFit covers 3 cities in Nevada: Las Vegas (204 locations), Reno (66 locations), Henderson (27 locations).

Where can an RV or box truck park in Nevada?

67 indexed facilities in the Nevada metro areas WillIFit covers are marked oversized-vehicle-friendly, including Sunset Station — Open-Air Parking in Henderson, Galleria at Sunset Mall in Henderson, The District at Green Valley Ranch in Henderson. See each city page for the full list.

How is the clearance data verified?

17 of the 297 locations in Nevada are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 31 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 249 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
