Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Missouri?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in the Missouri metro areas it covers is 6'2" (74 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in St. Louis. Coverage follows each city's metro area, so a structure just across a state line can appear here. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which Missouri cities does WillIFit cover?

WillIFit covers 5 cities in Missouri: St. Louis (300 locations), Kansas City (200 locations), Branson (52 locations), Springfield (41 locations), Columbia (40 locations).

Where can an RV or box truck park in Missouri?

72 indexed facilities in the Missouri metro areas WillIFit covers are marked oversized-vehicle-friendly, including Old 63 Parking Lot in Columbia, Upper Parking Lot in Columbia, Riechmann Parking Lot in Columbia. See each city page for the full list.

How is the clearance data verified?

21 of the 633 locations in Missouri are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 6 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 606 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
