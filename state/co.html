Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Colorado?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in the Colorado metro areas it covers is 6'10" (82 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in Grand Junction. Coverage follows each city's metro area, so a structure just across a state line can appear here. That clears a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") and anything shorter, but not a low-roof cargo van (7'9") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which Colorado cities does WillIFit cover?

How is the clearance data verified?

16 of the 499 locations in Colorado are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 11 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 472 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the FHWA National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
