Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in West Virginia?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in West Virginia is 7'6" (90 inches) at Low on-bridge clearance: WV Route 251 in Wheeling. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

Which West Virginia cities does WillIFit cover?

WillIFit covers 4 cities in West Virginia: Huntington (31 locations), Charleston (18 locations), Wheeling (15 locations), Morgantown (13 locations).

Where can an RV or box truck park in West Virginia?

How is the clearance data verified?

1 of the 77 locations in West Virginia are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself.
