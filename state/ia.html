Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Iowa?
The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Iowa is 7'0" (84 inches) at Low clearance: BR MUD CREEK under LOCAL in Cedar Rapids. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which Iowa cities does WillIFit cover?
WillIFit covers 5 cities in Iowa: Des Moines (72 locations), Iowa City (27 locations), Davenport (25 locations), Cedar Rapids (21 locations), Sioux City (17 locations).
Where can an RV or box truck park in Iowa?
20 indexed facilities in Iowa are marked oversized-vehicle-friendly, including Wanatee Park Campground in Cedar Rapids, Morgan Creek Campground in Cedar Rapids, West Lake Park - Summit Campground in Davenport. See each city page for the full list.
How is the clearance data verified?
4 of the 162 locations in Iowa are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 5 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 153 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
