Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Indiana?

The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Indiana is 6'0" (72 inches) at Low clearance underpass (service) in Indianapolis. That clears a typical sedan (5'0"), but not a stock pickup or SUV (6'6") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.

How is the clearance data verified?
