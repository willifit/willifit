Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in North Carolina?
The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in North Carolina is 7'0" (84 inches) at Tunnel (service) in Durham. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which North Carolina cities does WillIFit cover?

How is the clearance data verified?
28 of the 373 locations in North Carolina are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. Another 7 were verified against a published source such as the facility's own website. The remaining 338 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
