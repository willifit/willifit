Frequently asked

What is the lowest bridge clearance in Idaho?
The lowest posted bridge or underpass clearance WillIFit tracks in Idaho is 8'0" (96 inches) at Low clearance: CDA PARKING GARAGE under FRONT AVENUE in Coeur d'Alene. That clears a low-roof cargo van (7'0") and anything shorter, but not a mid-roof cargo van (8'4") or anything taller. Measure your own vehicle; racks, AC units, and lifts add inches.
Which Idaho cities does WillIFit cover?
WillIFit covers 3 cities in Idaho: Boise (45 locations), Coeur d'Alene (9 locations), Idaho Falls (8 locations).

How is the clearance data verified?
1 of the 62 locations in Idaho are AI-verified: the posted clearance was read directly from the entrance sign in Google Street View using Claude Vision (Anthropic's image AI), and we store the exact Street View pano so you can open it and check the sign yourself. The remaining 61 are imported from OpenStreetMap and the U.S. National Bridge Inventory and are not individually verified. Always confirm at the posted sign before you drive.
