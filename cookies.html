Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This page lists every cookie and every piece of browser storage Willifit uses — by name, with what it does and how long it lasts. No categories-only summary, no "and similar technologies" hand-waving. If it's on your device because of us, it's in the table below.

The one-line version. Willifit sets exactly two cookies, both from Google Analytics, and you can switch them off from the Cookie preferences control in the footer of any page. Everything else on the list is data your own browser keeps for you — your last city, your vehicle height, your favorites — which never reaches us and never needs your consent.

What's actually set

Two things live on your device when you use Willifit, and they are legally and practically different, so the table separates them:

  • Cookies are small files your browser sends back to a server with every request. Ours come from Google Analytics.
  • Device storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) is a place your browser keeps data for a site. It is never attached to a request automatically. Nothing in it reaches our servers unless the page deliberately sends it, and for the keys below it doesn't.

Cloudflare Web Analytics sets no cookies

Willifit also runs Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and page-load timings. It is genuinely cookieless: it sets nothing on your device, does no fingerprinting, and cannot follow you to another site. That is why it does not appear in the table, and why it keeps running regardless of your cookie choice — there is nothing to consent to.

Why the distinction matters

People use "cookies" as shorthand for anything a site keeps on your machine, and the law mostly does too — the EU rules cover storing or reading any information on your device, cookie or not. But the same rules carve out an exemption, and it is a sensible one:

  • Strictly necessary and functional storage does not require consent. If you ask for a thing and the site has to remember something to give it to you, remembering it is part of doing what you asked. Saving your vehicle height so the fit verdicts work, keeping your favorites, holding a report until the network comes back, and recording that you said "no" to analytics all fall here. Asking permission to remember your own preference would be theater.
  • Analytics cookies do require consent in the EU, UK, EEA, and Switzerland, because measuring your behavior is something we want, not something you asked for. That is why the two _ga cookies are the only entries in the table you can switch off — and why, if you're in one of those regions, they are not set at all until you say yes.

Outside those regions, most U.S. state privacy laws take the opt-out approach instead: analytics may run by default, but you must be able to turn it off easily and permanently. So that's what we do — the same control, available to everyone, whichever way your local rules point.

We are describing how we've set this up, not making a legal claim about any particular statute. If you think we've drawn a line in the wrong place, tell us.

How to withdraw consent

Any of these works, and none of them breaks the site.

Use the Cookie preferences control

Every page has a Cookie preferences button in the footer. Open it and choose Reject. Google Analytics stops setting and reading its cookies from that moment on. Your choice is stored in willifit_cookie_consent on that device, and you can change your mind as often as you want — there's no cooling-off period and we won't re-ask you every visit to wear you down.

Because the choice lives on your device, it applies to that browser on that device. If you use Willifit on a phone and a laptop, set it in both.

Send Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control is a signal your browser can send automatically on your behalf so you don't have to hunt for an opt-out on every site. Firefox supports it natively; Brave and DuckDuckGo send it; there are extensions for Chrome and Safari. If we receive it, we treat it as an opt-out from analytics without you doing anything else.

Delete what's already there

Opting out stops new cookies; it does not reach back and erase ones already set. To clear those, delete cookies for willifit.ai in your browser settings. Be aware that "clear site data" is usually all-or-nothing — it will also wipe the device storage in the table above, including your favorites, your saved vehicle height, and any report still sitting in a queue. Export anything pending first.

Block it at the source

Google publishes a Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on that blocks Google Analytics everywhere, not just here. Most content blockers do the same. Your browser's own settings can also block third-party cookies wholesale or discard everything when you close a private window.

Changes to this policy

If we add, remove, or repurpose anything on this page, we update the table and move the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change means new analytics or a new third party gets access to your data, we'll ask again rather than assume an old yes still covers it.
